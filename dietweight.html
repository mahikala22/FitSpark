Weight Training Diet Plan

Fuel Your Workout with the Right Nutrition

💪 Pre-Workout Meal (1-2 hrs before training)

  • Grilled chicken with brown rice
  • Oatmeal with banana and peanut butter
  • Greek yogurt with honey and berries

🏋 Post-Workout Meal (within 1 hr after training)

  • Protein shake with banana
  • Scrambled eggs with whole wheat toast
  • Grilled salmon with quinoa

🍽 Balanced Meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

  • High protein source (chicken, fish, eggs, tofu)
  • Complex carbs (brown rice, oats, quinoa)
  • Healthy fats (avocados, nuts, olive oil)
  • Plenty of vegetables
💧 Pro Tip: Stay hydrated! Aim for at least 3 liters of water daily, especially on workout days.
