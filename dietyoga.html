Diet Plan for Yogasana & Meditation

Fuel your body and mind with mindful nutrition

Introduction

Maintaining a balanced and sattvic (pure) diet is essential for enhancing the benefits of Yogasana and Meditation. The following plan supports clarity, calmness, and vitality throughout the day.

🌅 Morning (Pre & Post Yoga)

  • Warm water with lemon or herbal tea (upon waking)
  • After yoga: Fresh fruit or smoothie (banana, apple, berries)
  • Light breakfast: Oats with nuts/seeds, or upma with vegetables
  • Avoid heavy, fried, or processed foods in the morning

🌞 Afternoon (Lunch)

  • Balanced sattvic meal: Rice or whole grains, dal (lentils), vegetables
  • Fresh salad with cucumber, carrot, tomato, and lemon juice
  • Avoid too much spice, oil, or meat products
  • Optional: Buttermilk or lemon water

🌇 Evening (Pre-Meditation)

  • Light snack: Handful of nuts, fruit, or herbal tea
  • Dinner: Light khichdi, steamed vegetables, or soup
  • Finish dinner at least 2 hours before meditation or sleep
  • Stay hydrated throughout the day
