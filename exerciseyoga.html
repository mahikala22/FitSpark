Yoga & Meditation Exercises

Yogasana Exercises

Sukhasana

Improves posture, calms the mind, and stretches the knees and ankles.

Vrukshasana

Strengthens legs and improves balance and concentration.

Trikonasana

Promotes strength, balance, and flexibility by forming a triangular shape with the body, engaging multiple muscle groups, and encouraging mental focus and stability.

Bhujangasana

Strengthens the back muscles, improves spinal flexibility, stimulates abdominal organs, and helps alleviate stress and fatigue.

Surya Namaskara

A series of poses that energize the body and mind.

Guided Meditation

10-Min Relaxation

Perfect for quick stress relief and emotional reset.
